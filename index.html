The bar ruler at the top: click sets the cursor, drag sets the loop region, double click removes it.

The velocity lane: drag across the stems to set how hard the notes are struck. With notes selected, only those change.

The pedal lane: drag to press the sustain pedal, right-drag (or Shift + drag) to lift it.

The keyboard shows the keys that sound: orange for a finger (stronger for a harder blow), green for strings held by the pedal alone. Click a key to play it; lower on the key is louder.

Humanize (next to the tempo) plays the notes like a person: two hands and their fingers (the fourth and fifth are weaker), a chord too wide for a hand is rolled, a line is played legato, the pedal goes down just after the keys; timing and loudness drift, a phrase moves on and eases off, the top note is brought out and comes a little early, and the song slows into its last note. The Humanize slider (Player panel) sets how much. Switched off, the notes are played exactly as written.

Import MIDI (menu, or drop a .mid file on the page) takes every track but the drums, with its sustain pedal. The song has one tempo: where the file changes tempo, the notes keep their place in time. A file that was played by a person is not humanized again. Export MIDI writes the notes as written; Export WAV renders what you hear.

Your song lives in the page address: copy it any time to share.
